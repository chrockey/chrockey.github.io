Chunghyun Park

Hi! I'm Chunghyun (pronounced choong-hyun), a postdoc at POSTECH with my PhD advisor, Prof. Minsu Cho. I study 3D perception and dexterous manipulation. My research explores how robots can understand 3D environments and anticipate the effects of their actions to perform dexterous manipulation. Previously, I interned at NVIDIA, received the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship, and presented at the CVPR 2026 Doctoral Consortium.
